Support

Help with HLTH track.

Answers to the common questions, and how to reach us if yours isn’t here.

The app

What does HLTH track do?

It is a companion app for the HLTH band. The band has no screen, so the app is where its readings show up: heart rate, sleep, blood oxygen, steps, a stress score and a blood pressure estimate.

Which band does it work with?

The HLTH band. HLTH track is an independent app and is not made by the band’s maker.

Is it a medical device?

No. Readings and estimates are for general wellness. They are not for diagnosing or treating any condition, and a blood pressure estimate from a wrist band is not a cuff reading.

Privacy

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Open the app and connect your band.

Where are my readings kept?

Only on your phone, encrypted. They are never sent to us or anyone else. The privacy policy has the detail.

What happens when I get a new phone?

Because readings live only on your phone, the app does not carry them over. If Apple Health sync was on, the readings you shared are in Apple Health. Your purchase does come with you: tap Restore a purchase.

Price

What does it cost?

The app is free for 7 days. After that, one payment unlocks it for good. There is no subscription and nothing renews by itself. The app shows the exact price for your country before you start.

What happens if I don’t pay after the trial?

The app stops connecting to your band. The readings already on your phone stay yours to see, and nothing is charged.

I paid, but the app asks me to unlock again.

In the app, tap Restore a purchase. You need to be signed in to the same Apple account you bought it with, and online for a moment.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The band

The app can’t find my band.

Make sure the band has some charge, is within arm’s reach, and that Bluetooth is on and allowed for HLTH track in your phone’s Settings. Taking the band off its charger and putting it on usually wakes it.

A reading says “Didn’t finish” or “No reading”.

The band needs steady contact with your skin. Wear it snug, about a finger’s width above the wrist bone, keep still, and try again.

My band buzzes for calls and messages.

HLTH track switches those alerts off each time it connects. If another app for the band is installed, it may switch them back on.

Still stuck?

Email apostopher@gmail.com and say what you tried. We read every message.
