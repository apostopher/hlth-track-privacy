Privacy Policy

Your readings stay on your phone.

HLTH track is an app for reading the HLTH band. This policy says what the app does with your information.

Effective 16 November 2026

  • Health readings are stored only on your phone, encrypted.
  • They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No account, no sign-up and no email.
  • No ads and no tracking.
  • The one thing that goes online is your purchase.

Who we are

HLTH track is published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at apostopher@gmail.com.

HLTH track is not made by, or affiliated with, the maker of the HLTH band.

Your health readings

The app reads these from your band over Bluetooth: heart rate, sleep, blood oxygen, steps, stress scores and blood pressure estimates.

  • They are stored only on your phone. Readings are saved in the app’s own storage on your device, encrypted with a key that is kept on that device.
  • They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives readings, and the app does not upload them anywhere. We cannot see, share or sell them, because we never have them.
  • There is no account. The app does not ask for your name, email address or any sign-up.
  • There is no cloud backup from us. If you delete the app or change phones, readings stored in the app are not carried over by us.

Sharing with Apple Health or Health Connect

Sharing is off until you turn it on in Settings. If you turn it on, the app writes heart rate, sleep, blood oxygen and steps to Apple Health (on iPhone) or Health Connect (on Android). The app only writes to these stores; it does not read your other health data from them.

Data you send there is then handled under Apple’s or Google’s own terms. You can turn sharing off again at any time; readings already written stay in that store until you delete them there.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to find and talk to your band. It does not use Bluetooth to work out your location.

Purchases

HLTH track offers a free trial and a one-time purchase to unlock the app. This is the only time the app uses the internet.

  • Apple or Google handles the payment. We never see your card details, billing address or Apple or Google account.
  • RevenueCat keeps the record of the purchase. We use a service called RevenueCat to check whether the trial has started and whether the app has been unlocked. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for your installation of the app, the purchase or trial record from the App Store or Google Play, and basic technical details sent with any internet request, such as your device type, operating system version, app version, the country of your store account and your IP address.
  • No health readings are involved. Nothing the band measures is ever sent to RevenueCat, Apple, Google or us as part of a purchase.

You can read RevenueCat’s privacy policy, Apple’s and Google’s.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking of what you do in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Readings on your phone. You can remove a band and its readings from within the app, and deleting the app removes everything it has stored on your device.
  • Purchase records. These are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat so that a purchase can be restored on a new phone. To ask for the purchase record tied to your installation to be deleted, email apostopher@gmail.com. Because we do not know who you are, we may need details of the purchase to find the record.

Children

HLTH track is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not a medical device

HLTH track shows readings and estimates from a wellness band. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you have health concerns, talk to a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy
